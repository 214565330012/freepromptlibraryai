Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using the prompt library.

What is FreePromptLibraryAI?

FreePromptLibraryAI is a library of free AI photo editing and video prompt ideas. Browse a prompt, view its complete text and copy it for use in a supported creative app.

Are the prompts free to copy?

Yes. The prompt text shown in this starter library can be copied for your creative projects. Check the terms of the AI service you use.

Can I use these prompts with my own photo?

For photo-editing prompts, open a compatible image tool, upload your reference photo and paste the prompt. Follow the instructions shown by that tool.

Are English and Hindi versions available?

Supported prompts include English and Hindi text. Use the language selector in the prompt window to switch languages.

Which apps can I use?

Depending on the prompt, you can try compatible image or video prompts in Gemini, ChatGPT or another supported creative service.

How often are new prompts added?

New prompt collections are planned to be reviewed and published regularly as new creative trends emerge.
